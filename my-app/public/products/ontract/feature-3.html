Payment terms

Tenant will pay monthly base rent of USD $38,400 in year one, increasing by three percent (3%) on each anniversary of the commencement date. Rent is due on the first day of each month.

Termination clause

Tenant has one option to renew for an additional three (3) years at the then-prevailing market rate, exercisable by written notice delivered no later than one hundred twenty (120) days before expiration.

Key obligations

  • Tenant will pay monthly base rent.
  • Tenant will pay its proportionate share (8.2%) of increases in building operating expenses over the 2024 base year.
  • Tenant maintains the interior of the Premises in good condition.
  • Tenant will maintain commercial general liability insurance of at least $2,000,000 per occurrence, naming Landlord as an additional insured.
